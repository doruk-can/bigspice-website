<a id="on-your-device"></a>
## 3. What stays on your device

The following data is stored in the App's own storage on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers as part of normal use:

- your recipe library, including recipes you import or create, and any cover photo you choose;
- your cooking sessions, your history and your cooking statistics;
- your shopping list;
- your preferences, your region and your measurement settings;
- a short list of ingredients you recently told the App you have;
- whether you have given the AI Features permission (section 4.1), and when; and
- a copy of your Pro status, so that the App works without a connection.

This has three consequences.

- **We cannot see it.** We cannot read, recover or delete data that exists only on your device.
- **You control it.** You can delete items in the App, or erase everything at once with "Delete my data" in Settings (section 11.3). Deleting the App from your device removes the data the App stored there. Deleting a recipe also deletes the voice clips saved for it.
- **Backups are yours.** If you have switched on device backups with the maker of your device, the App's data may be included in them. We do not control those backups.

Parts of this data leave your device only when you use a feature that needs them. The main examples are importing a recipe and Ask the Chef, described in section 4.

<a id="ai-features"></a>
## 4. How AI Features handle what you submit

AI Features include importing a recipe from a web link, a video link or a photo; Ask the Chef; and adding ingredients from a photo.

**4.1 What is sent, and when.**

**The App asks your permission before it sends anything to an AI Feature for the first time. It tells you what will be sent and who will receive it. Nothing is sent until you tap "Allow and continue".** If you tap "Not now", nothing is sent, the rest of the App keeps working, and the App asks again the next time you try. The App keeps your answer on your device. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings, under "Your data". We will ask again if the kinds of data that are sent, or the kinds of recipient, change in a way that matters.

- **A link.** The App sends the link to our servers. Our servers fetch the page, or the text of the video, for you.
- **A photo.** You can choose a photo from your gallery. For a photo of your fridge or your ingredients, you can take one with the camera instead. Where your device supports it, the App makes the photo smaller on your device before sending it. The photo is then sent to our servers. When you add ingredients from a photo, the App also sends the language you read in, so that the ingredient names come back in your language.
- **With any import.** The App also sends the number of people you cook for, so that the recipe comes back sized for your household, and the language you read in.
- **Ask the Chef.** The App sends your request, including anything you type, the ingredients you chose, the things you want to avoid, and the preferences that matter for the answer: household size, diet, dislikes, kitchen equipment, cooking goals and the language you read in.

**4.2 Who processes it.** Our servers process what you submit, together with AI and content-processing service providers that act for us. Content-processing providers do jobs such as getting the text of a video. **When our servers pass your request on to these providers, they do not include your account identifier or your IP address.**

**4.3 What we do with it, and what we do not do.**

- We use what you submit only to produce the result you asked for, to apply fair usage limits and to keep the service secure.
- We do not sell it.
- We do not use it for advertising.
- We do not keep it on our servers after your result has been produced. Our technical logs record details such as the size of a request and whether it succeeded, together with your account identifier and your IP address. They do not record the content itself.
- We do not use it to train AI models of our own.

Our AI and content-processing service providers handle what we send them under their terms with us. They may keep it for a period, for example for safety and abuse monitoring. You can ask us for more information at info@ryonlabs.com.

**4.4 What you should not submit.** AI Features do not need personal information. Please do not submit any. Photos should show food, ingredients or a recipe, not people or documents. A photo file can carry hidden details, such as when and where it was taken. In some cases those details are sent with the photo. We do not read or use them.

**4.5 Diet settings are cooking preferences.** We do not ask why you chose a diet setting or a dislike. We do not use these settings to work out anything about your health or your beliefs. Please do not tell the App about medical conditions.

**4.6 Automated results.** The results of AI Features are produced automatically and can be wrong. Our Terms of Use explain the risks. AI Features produce recipes. They do not make decisions about you. We do not make decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you by automated means alone.

## 11. Your rights

**11.1 How to use your rights.** You can erase your data yourself in the App (section 11.3). For everything else, write to info@ryonlabs.com. Using your rights is free. We answer within the time the law sets. That is usually one month. It is 30 days in Turkey and 45 days in California.

To erase your data you do not need us to find you: "Delete my data" works from inside the App (section 11.3).

<a id="deleting-your-data"></a>
**11.3 Deleting your data.**

- **In the App.** Open Settings, go to "Your data" and tap "Delete my data". It works for everyone, and you do not need to contact us. The App tells you what will happen and asks you to confirm. **It cannot be undone.**
- **What it erases on your device.** Your saved and imported recipes and their pictures, your cooking history and statistics, your shopping list, your preferences and settings, the voice clips saved on your device, the record of your AI Features permission, and any cooking timers that are waiting to alert you.
- **What it erases on our servers.** The personal records held against your account identifier: the profile fields (including a name, a picture or an email address, if we ever held one), any preferences and any notification tokens. The account is then marked as deleted.
- **What happens next.** The App signs out. It then restarts, or asks you to reopen it, and starts again as a new install with a new account identifier. If our servers cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and the App tells you so. Try again when you are online.
- **What we keep.** Some records are kept after a deletion, because erasing them would break the protection they exist for. These are the records that apply fair usage limits and stop them being abused, the records that let a purchase be restored, and the records that we need to meet legal obligations or to handle legal claims. In this version of the App these records contain the random account identifier, the kind of action, its date and an internal reference, your usage allowance, your purchase status and identifiers of subscription events. They do not contain your name, your email address or anything you submitted. We keep only what those purposes need.
- **What the button does not reach.** Analytics events and crash reports that our providers have already received stay with them until their retention period ends (section 9). After the button has run, the App uses a new account identifier and a new analytics identifier, so new events are not connected to the old ones. Our subscription management provider keeps its record of your purchase. Our technical logs run out on their own schedule (section 9). The button also does not reach messages you sent us, copies in backups you made with your device's own tools, or anything our AI and content-processing service providers still hold under section 4.3.
- **Your subscription.** Deleting data does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in your app store account settings. To use Pro again after you delete your data, tap "Restore purchases". Restoring does not give you a new usage allowance.
- **If you deleted the App without doing this.** The data on your device went with the App. Deleting the App does not erase the server records. You can ask us by email to delete them (next point).
- **By email.** You can also write to info@ryonlabs.com and ask us to delete the server records connected to your account identifier. Section 11.2 explains why we may need more information from you first.

## 13. Photos and device permissions

- **Photos.** When you add a photo, the App opens the photo picker of your device and you choose a photo. The App receives only the photos you choose. It does not get access to the rest of your photo library. For a photo of your fridge or your ingredients, you can take one with the camera instead.
